# 2 · WHAT THE PRO PAYS ON A JOB

## Residential (homeowner jobs)

**Flat percentage set by tier — 15% / 10% / 9% / 8%.**

- Charged to **the pro**, collected **after the pro is paid**
- **Never visible to the homeowner** and never changes the homeowner's price
- Charged on the **full quoted amount, including materials** (this matches how trade sales commissions work)
- **The fee does not come back to the pro.** No rebate, no keep rate

## Where the fee goes (the "commission pool")

| Claim | Share of the fee | When |
|---|---|---|
| Home / photo origination | 5% | **Founding network only** |
| Upline Level 1 | 7% | **Founding network only**, residential + Scout jobs only |
| Upline Level 2 | 4% | same |
| Upline Level 3 | 2% | same |
| Upline Level 4 | 1% | same |
| **ProLnk retains** | **the rest** | always |

- **No referrer and no origination claim → ProLnk keeps 100%.** This is the normal case
- **Maximum payout is 19%**, so ProLnk retains at least **81%** in the worst case
- The **≥20% retention floor** stays as a safety mechanism but can no longer trigger
- **Effective ProLnk take on a residential job: 8.1%–10%** of job value at Solo tier

## Commercial

| | |
|---|---|
| Work from the licensee who invited them | **The pro pays nothing** — the licensee is paying for the platform |
| Commercial work **ProLnk sources outside** the licensee | **3% of job value** |
| Cap on large jobs | **None currently.** A step-down above $25K was considered and not adopted |

**The residential 6–15% clamp does not apply to commercial.** Commercial needs its own fee path in code.

## Rental work orders

**The pro pays nothing.** The rental company pays **$3 per work order**.

# 3 · ORGANIZATIONS (operators, licensees, builders)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Annual license — large operators** | **$150,000–300,000** depending on portfolio |
| **Per work order** | **$3**, paid by the operator |
| **Small operators (under ~10,000 doors)** | **$1.50–2.50 per door per month, with no revenue share** |
| **Revenue share — operators over 10,000 doors** | **20%** |

## What the 20% applies to

**Everything:** partner streams (insurance, utility connections, commerce), **vendor subscriptions** from vendors they bring, and **data licensing** on their properties.

- **Flat 20%, not tiered.** This supersedes the earlier 30% and the tiered-to-30%-ceiling data arrangement
- **Split at settlement and paid partner-direct.** It never enters ProLnk's revenue or costs, so there is no remittance ledger and ProLnk's share is 100% margin
- **The 20% is the price of becoming their resident platform** — not a marketing incentive

# 5 · FOUNDING NETWORK

**Capped at 2,125 FIRMS — companies, not individuals. Never marketed publicly.**

| Tier | Cap |
|---|---|
| **Charter** | 25 |
| **Founding** | 100 |
| **Cornerstone** | 400 |

- **The lock covers the base subscription only.** Additional ZIP codes, ProPasses, dashboard seats, and the commercial upgrade are charged at **current pricing** and are **subject to future increases**
- Override applies to **residential homeowner jobs and Scout jobs only** — never commercial, never rental work orders

## What the founding price lock actually covers

| Locked at $149/mo for life | Charged at current pricing, and can increase |
|---|---|
| Base subscription | Additional ZIP codes ($25 / 10 ZIPs) |
| Business-tier features | Additional ProPasses ($20 each) |

**The lock is on the base rate, not the account.** A founding firm that grows to 20 technicians pays $149 plus 19 ProPasses at whatever the rate is then. That keeps the promise honest and keeps your exposure capped — 2,125 base subscriptions, not 2,125 unlimited accounts.

# 6 · HOMEOWNERS AND RESIDENTS

**Free. Always. Both of them.**

- Homeowners never pay for the app, and never see the platform fee
- Residents never pay for the app
- **Residents do pay for services they personally buy** (cleaning, mounting) — they see that price at checkout. They never see vendor names, operator-paid maintenance prices, or quote comparisons
- **Care plans are deferred.** Do not market or price them
