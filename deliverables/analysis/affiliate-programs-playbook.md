# Affiliate Programs Playbook — ProLnk / TrustyPro

**Date:** August 17, 2026
**Why feeds matter:** ProLnk's AI room renderings need bulk product data (images, prices, SKUs). Programs with **product catalog feeds or product APIs** are strategically more valuable than ones with only tracked links — feed availability is weighted heavily below.
**Rate caveat:** Commission rates below come from program pages and affiliate-industry aggregators; aggregator-sourced figures are marked *(unverified)* and can change — confirm inside each network dashboard at signup.

Key details:

- **Wayfair** — 5–7% on all orders, ~$300 AOV, 7-day cookie, and the program explicitly includes **data feeds** ([nichepursuits.com](https://www.nichepursuits.com/wayfair-affiliate-program/), [getlasso.co](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/wayfair/), [affiliateprograms.com](https://affiliateprograms.com/p/wayfair/)). The single best feed-plus-commission combination for furniture renders.
- **Amazon Associates** — Furniture/Home at **3%** ([azonpress.com](https://azonpress.com/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates/), [helpingmerchants.com](https://helpingmerchants.com/amazon-affiliate-commission-rates-by-category/)). Approval mechanics: you must generate **3 qualifying sales within 180 days** of joining or the account closes ([azonpress requirements guide](https://azonpress.com/key-amazon-affiliate-requirements/)). **Product API caution (important, time-sensitive):** research indicates **PA-API 5.0 was retired in May 2026** and its successor (a Creators/OAuth-based API) requires roughly **10 qualified sales in the trailing 30 days** for access — up from the old 3-sale rule ([keywordrush.com on the AssociateNotEligible error / new 10-sales rule](https://www.keywordrush.com/blog/amazon-pa-api-associatenoteligible-error-is-there-a-new-10-sales-rule/), [dev.to PA-API 2026 status](https://dev.to/agenthustler/amazon-product-api-pa-api-in-2026-restrictions-alternatives-and-web-scraping-4l35), [Amazon requirements page](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GVJ2BJP35457CLML); *verify current terms in the Associates dashboard*). Practical consequence: **start the account and the sales clock NOW** — API access for the renderer only unlocks after sustained sales volume. Mobile apps require separate approval in the Associates dashboard (list the app under Mobile Apps; policy may require the resident app to be listed in the Amazon Appstore — confirm during application).
- **Home Depot** — runs on Impact; commissions are low single digits for most categories with appliances often excluded/reduced ([getlasso.co](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/home-depot/), [geniuslink.com](https://geniuslink.com/blog/home-depot-affiliate-program/)). Impact provides product catalogs/datafeeds to approved partners.
- **Lowe's** — ~2%, 1-day cookie *(unverified)* ([getlasso.co](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/lowes/), [geniuslink.com](https://geniuslink.com/blog/lowes-affiliate-program/)); also a separate [Lowe's Creator program](https://www.lowes.com/l/creator/joinlowescreator) aimed at influencers.
- **Target** — [affiliate.target.com](https://affiliate.target.com/), up to ~8% on Home & Outdoor with volume tiers, 7-day cookie *(unverified aggregate: [diggitymarketing.com](https://diggitymarketing.com/best-affiliate-programs/target/), [tapfiliate.com](https://tapfiliate.com/blog/target-affiliate-program/))*.
- **Bed Bath & Beyond / Overstock (Beyond Inc.)** — BB&B affiliate page states up to 3% US sales via **Partnerize** ([bedbathandbeyond.com/affiliates](https://www.bedbathandbeyond.com/affiliates)); Overstock legacy listings show up to 6% on CJ *(unverified — the Beyond brand consolidation makes older listings unreliable; [uppromote roundup](https://uppromote.com/blog/home-decor-furniture-affiliate-programs/))*.
- **Article** — modern DTC furniture; appears in home-decor affiliate roundups but publishes no rates ([afftank roundup](https://afftank.com/blog/home-decor-furniture-affiliate-programs)); apply and ask about feeds — DTC catalogs are small but photography is excellent for renders.
- **Ashley** — sources conflict badly (7% per [getlasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/ashley-homestore/) vs 0.68% per [Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/ashleyfurniture-affiliate-program)) — *treat as unverified*; possibly different regional programs. Worth an application to see real terms; Ashley's Texas footprint fits our markets.

## 2. Nebraska Furniture Mart — no public affiliate program

**Confirmed: no affiliate program on any major network** ([Knoji check of CJ/Rakuten/Pepperjam/ShareASale](https://nfm.knoji.com/questions/nebraska-furniture-mart-affiliate-programs/)). They do run an **[NFM Influencer Partner Network](https://nfm.knoji.com/questions/nfm-influencer-marketing/)** and a [Partner Network page](https://www.nfm.com/partnernetwork.html), and have precedent for creative retail partnerships ([Amazon store-within-a-store](https://www.twice.com/retailing/nfm-amazon-partner-for-exclusive-store-within-a-store), [KC Monarchs sponsorship](http://monarchsbaseball.com/monarchs-partner-with-nebraska-furniture-mart/)).

**Direct-partnership pitch (the play):**
- **Who:** NFM e-commerce/digital marketing leadership (Omaha HQ) via the Partner Network page; warm-up via their Texas store leadership (The Colony, TX — Dallas metro) since ProLnk's launch markets overlap.
- **The offer:** ProLnk's AI room renderings furnish new residents' actual floor plans with NFM SKUs at move-in — the highest-intent furniture moment that exists. We need: catalog feed (images/prices/SKUs), a tracked-link or promo-code attribution scheme, and a negotiated referral fee (anchor at 5–7%, Wayfair parity).
- **Why they'll care:** NFM's model is regional dominance (TX/KS/NE/IA) — exactly our Sun Belt/Texas render markets; a Berkshire Hathaway retailer with no affiliate channel gets a measurable new-mover acquisition channel with zero cannibalization.
- **Fallback:** run Wayfair/Amazon feeds in renders now; slot NFM in when the deal lands.

## 3. Appliances

- **Home Depot & Lowe's** — see above; note both historically pay reduced/zero commission on appliances specifically ([geniuslink Home Depot notes](https://geniuslink.com/blog/home-depot-affiliate-program/)) — confirm category schedules in-dashboard.
- **Best Buy** — program exists via **Impact** but pays **~0.5–1%, often 0% for returning customers, 1-day cookie** ([getlasso.co](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/best-buy/), [official program page](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/misc/best-buy-affiliate-program/pcmcat198500050002.c?id=pcmcat198500050002)). The **[Best Buy Developer API](https://developer.bestbuy.com/)** (products, stores, categories — [docs](https://bestbuyapis.github.io/api-documentation/)) still exists, but new API-key issuance is reportedly restricted/slow in 2026 — apply, but treat the **Impact datafeed** (available with affiliate approval) as the reliable product-data path for appliance/electronics renders.

## 4. Moving

- **HireAHelper** — has a real [affiliate program](https://www.hireahelper.com/affiliate/) (moving-labor marketplace, part of the Porch Group family); rates on application. Best-fit product for renters (labor-only moves).
- **U-Haul** — **no traditional affiliate program**; their model is [Moving Help®](https://www.movinghelper.com/) (service-provider marketplace) and dealer programs ([U-Haul's own comparison of Moving Help vs HireAHelper](https://www.uhaul.com/Tips/Moving-Help/Moving-Help-Vs-Hireahelper-Best-Moving-Labor-Marketplace-41565/)). Treat U-Haul as a future direct partnership, not an affiliate signup.
- **MovingAPT / long-distance broker programs** — MovingAPT advertises affiliate/referral arrangements for lead generation (site claims; *unverified terms*). Moving lead-gen generally pays per-lead ($5–50/lead industry norm, *unverified*) — e.g., [MoveAdvisor's affiliate pitch](https://moveadvisor.com/biz/affiliate). Vet brokers carefully — moving lead-gen quality varies and a bad mover reflects on TrustyPro.

- **CubeSmart** — affiliate arrangement reported to exist ([Knoji](https://cubesmartselfstorage.knoji.com/questions/cubesmart-self-storage-affiliate-programs/), *unverified*); redundant if SpareFoot is signed.

### Application cheat sheet — paste-ready answers

**Universal fields (prep once):**
- **Business name:** LIT Ventures LLC *(or the exact registered entity — must match W-9)* d/b/a ProLnk
- **Website URL:** https://prolnk.com *(use the live marketing site; most networks reject apps without a functioning site — make sure it has visible content + privacy policy before applying)*
- **Tax ID:** EIN `XX-XXXXXXX` *(W-9 info requested at or shortly after approval on every network)*
- **Traffic/promotional description (use verbatim):** "Mobile app and website serving renters and new movers in Sun Belt markets (TX, GA, FL, AZ, NC). We provide AI-powered room design/visualization, moving checklists, and home-services booking; we promote products via in-app recommendations and editorial content. No coupon/incentive traffic, no brand-term bidding."
- **Monthly traffic estimate:** answer honestly; "under 10k, launching" is acceptable everywhere except it may slow Wayfair review.

**Per-program specifics:**

1. **Amazon Associates** — [affiliate-program.amazon.com](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/) — sign in with a clean Amazon account; list BOTH the website and the mobile app (apps go under "Mobile Apps," need to be live in an app store for approval); pick payment by deposit; topics: Home & Garden. **Clock starts: 3 qualifying sales in 180 days** or account closes ([requirements](https://azonpress.com/key-amazon-affiliate-requirements/)); apply for **PA-API** from the Associates dashboard after first sales land.
2. **Wayfair** — apply through **CJ Affiliate** ([program overview](https://www.nichepursuits.com/wayfair-affiliate-program/)): create CJ publisher account → add "media property" (site + app) with the traffic description above → apply to Wayfair North America advertiser. After approval, request the **product datafeed** subscription in CJ.
3. **Home Depot** — apply through **Impact** ([program info](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/home-depot/)): create Impact partner account → same media-property setup → apply to The Home Depot; then request catalog/datafeed access in Impact's Catalogs section.
4. **SpareFoot** — via **FlexOffers** ([listing](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/sparefoot-storage-affiliate-program/)): FlexOffers publisher signup (site URL + description above) → search "SpareFoot Storage" → apply.
5. **HireAHelper** — direct at [hireahelper.com/affiliate](https://www.hireahelper.com/affiliate/): short form — business name, site, contact, promotion description (use the paste text; emphasize "moving checklist product surfaces moving-labor booking at the moment of lease signing").

**One-time prep (15 min):** confirm the exact legal entity + EIN to use everywhere; ensure prolnk.com has a privacy policy page including affiliate-disclosure language (Amazon requires the Associates disclosure verbatim on the site/app before payout).
