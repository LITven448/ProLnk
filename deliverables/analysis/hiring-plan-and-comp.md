# Hiring Plan — Who, Where to Find Them, What to Pay
**August 2026. Comp benchmarks are US venture-backed market ranges; equity ranges assume seed/Series A stage.**

---

## The honest correction first

**Do not hire a leader for each business line.** Commercial, homeowner, rental, and pro are not four businesses — they are one platform with four customer types. Hiring four GMs creates four fiefdoms, four roadmap agendas, and roughly $2M of burn before there is revenue to support any of it.

**One sales leader and one partnerships leader covers commercial, rental, and pro for at least two years.**

**And the first deals must be founder-sold.** Selling CoolSys and AMH personally is not cost-saving — it is how you learn what to systematize, what objections recur, and what the product actually needs. A VP Sales hired before that learning exists will spend a year rediscovering it at $400K.

**Current bottleneck check:** the constraint today is the 120-day build and founder time, not sales headcount. Hiring sellers now would put people in front of a product that isn't ready.

## Where each person actually comes from

The talent pools are specific and small. Search LinkedIn by company, current or former.

| Function | Hire from |
|---|---|
| **Commercial / facilities sales** | ServiceChannel, Corrigo (JLL), Ecotrak, FEXA, Accruent, ServiceTitan, Lessen/SMS Assist, Verisk. They already know the buyers and the objections |
| **Rental / operator side** | Yardi, RealPage, AppFolio, Entrata, Roofstock — **or from an operator itself** (AMH, Invitation Homes, Progress). Someone who has *been* the buyer is worth more than someone who has sold to them |

## How to source without paying a recruiter early

- **Contingency recruiters cost 20–25% of first-year salary; retained search runs $80–120K.** Avoid both until VP-level and post-raise.
- **Advisors refer.** The single best sourcing channel — a fractional advisor from Verisk knows every data person worth hiring. This is half the reason to bring advisors on early.
- **Investors' talent networks.** A real benefit of the right lead investor; ask about it during diligence, it is a legitimate selection criterion.
- **Bolster** — a marketplace built specifically for fractional executives and board members. Also **Continuum** and **Graphite** for fractional senior operators.
- **Hire the buyer.** For the operator side especially, recruiting someone out of AMH or Invitation Homes buys domain credibility no salesperson can fake.

## Equity guardrails
- Total option pool at Series A: negotiate **7–8%**, not the lazy 15% investors will propose. Size it to an actual 18-month hiring plan — this is the most negotiable and least negotiated line in a term sheet
- VP-level 0.5–1.5% · Director 0.25–0.5% · Senior IC 0.05–0.25% · Advisor 0.1–0.5%
- Standard four-year vest, one-year cliff, no exceptions for advisors or fractional help
