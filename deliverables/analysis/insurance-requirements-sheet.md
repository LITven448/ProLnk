## Questions to ask the broker
1. Does the E&O policy cover claims arising from AI-generated assessments/recommendations? (Get it in writing.)
2. Are independent-contractor (1099) service pros excluded? What endorsement covers negligent-vetting claims?
3. Does cyber cover regulatory defense (CCPA/TDPSA) and voluntary notification?
4. Can limits be increased mid-term when an enterprise contract (AMH) requires it, and at what pro-rated cost?
5. Additional-insured endorsements for enterprise customers — included or per-certificate fee?
