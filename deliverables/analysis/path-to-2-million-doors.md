# PART 3 — STRATEGY: SEQUENCING THE NEXT TWO QUARTERS

## The proof cascade
Everything sequences off the **AMH pilot**. Before approaching anyone else at scale you need, from AMH: (1) resident activation %, (2) maintenance cost per door vs. control, (3) triage deflection rate (issues resolved without dispatch), (4) Move-In Shield dispute/claim outcomes, (5) resident NPS delta. Public REITs will ask for exactly these; PE owners will ask for #2 alone.

## Next 2 quarters — 5 rental targets
1. **American Homes 4 Rent** — close it. The reference logo everything else cites.
2. **Amherst / Main Street Renewal (Austin)** — nearest large SFR peer geographically; PE economics; validates a *second* SFR operator so AMH isn't a one-off.
3. **Asset Living (Houston)** — the multifamily beachhead. 455K units, fee-manager economics, TX HQ. This single signature is ~45% of the rental goal.
4. **Camden Property Trust (Houston)** — first multifamily *owner-REIT* proof; public-company validation that unlocks MAA and the AVB/EQR combination later.
5. **VineBrook Homes** — small, fast, and the highest-pain book (old Midwest stock, habitability scrutiny). Generates the most dramatic before/after maintenance numbers of anyone — your best case-study data even if the smallest logo.

*(Deliberately deferred: Greystar and Invitation Homes — both have internal-build instincts; approach them in Q1–Q2 2027 with two operators' worth of published results, or risk teaching their product teams your roadmap. AVB/EQR: wait for merger close, then hit the combined ops org early in integration.)*

## Next 2 quarters — 5 homeowner targets
1. **Highland Homes (Plano)** — the builder pilot. Private, TX, fast yes/no. Produces the "warranty calls down X%" number every national builder will demand.
2. **D.R. Horton (Arlington)** — start the relationship now (long cycle), armed with Highland pilot by close. Also a bridge: they sell communities to SFR operators you've already signed.
3. **Rocket / Mr. Cooper** — the 10M-homeowner conversation. Urgent for a defensive reason too: Rocket is explicitly assembling a homeownership platform (Redfin + Mr. Cooper, 2025) — they are the most likely company in America to *build* your owner-side product. Partner before they do.
4. **Associa (Dallas)** — HOA channel anchor, hometown HQ, 2.3M units, and dual-sided (touches rentals inside HOAs too).
5. **Texas Farm Bureau Insurance (or Germania)** — small-carrier pilot to generate the loss-prevention actuarial data (claims frequency on app-active homes) that State Farm/USAA/Progressive will require. Carrier validation is a 2-quarter data-collection exercise — start the clock now.

## Exclusive-with-each-other vs. stackable
- **Stackable (pursue in parallel, no conflict):** builders + HOA managers + one carrier + one servicer + security. They touch the same homeowner at different moments (closing, move-in, policy renewal) — overlap is fine; first activation wins the account, dedupe by address.
- **Effectively exclusive (pick one per category, at least initially):**
  - **Warranty:** Frontdoor vs. Cinch vs. First American — each will demand exclusivity because triage deflection is their core margin lever, and each views the others as direct enemies. Choosing Frontdoor likely forecloses Cinch's white-label network; weigh 2M direct members vs. Cinch's distribution multiplier before signing.
  - **Servicer:** a Rocket deal will almost certainly carry exclusivity or MFN terms vs. other servicers; a deep Rocket partnership also complicates brokerage deals with Compass/KW (Rocket owns Redfin).
  - **Multifamily managers:** not legally exclusive, but Greystar/Asset Living/Willow Bridge compete for the same management contracts — expect "don't make us the demo for our rival" pressure. Sequence, don't parallel-pitch, the top 3.
  - **Within one metro's SFR book:** no conflict at all — operators don't share residents. Sign every SFR operator you can.
- **Channel-conflict watch:** Frontdoor (warranty) vs. carriers (insurance perks) both monetize the same repair event — if you sign Frontdoor exclusively, structure carrier deals as *referral* not warranty-replacement, or you'll be arbitrating your own partners.
