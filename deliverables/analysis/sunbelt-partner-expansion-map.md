# Sunbelt Partner Expansion Map — ProLnk / TrustyPro

**Prepared:** August 2026
**Scope:** Catalog partners (product feeds for Shop + AI room-rendering engine) and service partners (move-in/move-out and recurring home-services moments) for the Sunbelt rollout: **TX first → GA, FL, AZ, NC, TN, NV**.

**How to read this document**
- **Catalog partner gating requirement:** a machine-readable product feed (SKUs, images, prices, dimensions). No feed = no rendering-engine integration, regardless of commission.
- **Priority tiers:** **A** = approach in the next 60 days · **B** = after TX pilot proves conversion · **C** = at scale (multi-state, volume leverage).
- Commission figures come from public affiliate directories/network listings as of mid-2026 and are marked **(unverified)** where no reliable public figure exists. All rates should be re-confirmed inside the network dashboards (CJ, Impact, Partnerize, FlexOffers, Awin, AvantLink) before modeling revenue.
- "Direct-pitch-only" = no public affiliate program; the path is a bespoke partnership (rendering placement, move-in bundle referrals, trade-program adaptation).

# (c) The 3 regional furniture players where a DIRECT pitch is most likely to land

The pitch asset in all three cases: **rendering placement + move-in-moment offers across a ~60K-home, Texas-heavy rental portfolio** — distribution they cannot buy anywhere else, aimed at customers (new renters) at the exact moment furniture demand peaks.

**1. Nebraska Furniture Mart (The Colony, TX)**
*Hook:* "Your Colony store is a 560,000-sq-ft showroom that people drive an hour to visit — we put it inside the phone of every renter moving into DFW, the week they get their keys." NFM has no affiliate program to hide behind, a demonstrated appetite for novel retail partnerships (the Amazon store-within-a-store, Shaw galleries), and — uniquely on this list — sells **furniture + appliances + electronics + flooring**, meaning one NFM deal fills an entire AI-rendered room. Berkshire-owned, so pitch ROI discipline: pay-per-delivered-order, no media spend.

**2. Rooms To Go (FL HQ; stores in TX, FL, GA, NC, TN)**
*Hook:* "Your whole-room packages are already the answer to the question our renderer asks — 'what does this empty room look like furnished, for one price, delivered this week?' We render *your* rooms into *their* actual apartment." RTG's package merchandising maps 1:1 onto whole-room AI renders, its footprint covers five of ProLnk's seven launch states, and it already operates a (thin) affiliate program — proof the partnerships function exists; the direct pitch upgrades it from links to in-render placement with move-in-date-triggered delivery.

**3. Gallery Furniture (Houston)**
*Hook:* "Mattress Mack furnishes Houston same-day — we hand you every Houston renter on the day they sign a lease, and your same-day delivery closes the render-to-living-room loop before any national competitor can ship a box." Gallery is a single-owner decision (Jim McIngvale personally does deals fast), has legendary local trust and a promotional-partnership history (Kroger, Caesars, City of Houston), and its same-day delivery is the only fulfillment in Texas that matches the emotional peak of move-in day. Small SKU count makes manual catalog onboarding a weekend project, and a Gallery win is a Houston-market PR story on its own.

*(Runner-up: Living Spaces — arguably the best technical fit due to existing 3D assets, but it's a bigger corporate machine; it's in the 60-day approach list as a standard partnerships pitch rather than a founder-to-founder direct play.)*

## Critical corrections to prior planning assumptions

1. **Conn's HomePlus is dead.** Chapter 11 July 2024, all ~170 stores closed by Oct 31, 2024, Badcock wound down too. Remove from every deck and model. BrandsMart USA (FL/GA) is the closest living substitute for the value-appliance slot.
2. **Ashley online affiliate pays ~1%** — do not model Ashley e-comm as a meaningful revenue line; route Ashley product through licensee relationships (Mathis owns Ashley stores) or a direct manufacturer conversation.
3. **Costco is not a catalog partner** — membership bounties only ($3/$6), session cookie, no product feed.
4. **Best Buy economics (0.5–1%, 1-day cookie, new customers only)** make it a coverage listing at best.
5. **AHS affiliate terms exclude real-estate-transaction leads** — a rental-platform deal must be structured as a direct partnership with Frontdoor, not run through the public affiliate program.
6. **At Home and Kirkland's both carry restructuring risk (2025 events, unverified details)** — confirm corporate stability before engineering investment.
