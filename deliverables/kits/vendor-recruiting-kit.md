**3. New demand beyond the portfolio.**
ProLnk also serves homeowners and other properties in your area. Once you're verified, you can opt into jobs beyond [OPERATOR_NAME]'s portfolio — same app, same payout rails.

1. **Accept your invite** — 10 minutes: business info, trades, service area.
2. **Get verified** — insurance cert, license (where required), background screening via Checkr, payout setup. Most vendors clear in 1–3 business days.
3. **Go live** — [OPERATOR_NAME] approves you, and work orders start routing to your app.

## 4. One-page vendor FAQ

*(Attach as PDF to Email 1 and host at prolnk.xyz/[operator-slug]/faq.)*

**What is this?**
[OPERATOR_NAME] dispatches, documents, and pays its vendors through ProLnk. You're invited because you're already one of their trusted vendors.

**What does it cost?**
Two options, your choice, switch anytime:
- **$99/mo (same as existing Core tier)** — unlimited work orders, no per-job fee.
- **$5–$8 per work order (vendor-charged; final TBD) per completed work order** — pay only when you work.
Signing up, verification, and seeing jobs are always free. There are **no per-lead fees** and no commission taken from your invoice for portfolio work orders.

**What do I need to get verified?**
1. Basic business info (name, EIN or SSN for 1099, trades, service area)
2. **Insurance:** general liability certificate, minimum [GL_MINIMUM] coverage, ProLnk listed as certificate holder
3. **License:** required for licensed trades in your state (plumbing, electrical, HVAC — [STATE_REQUIREMENTS])
4. **Background screening:** completed through Checkr (email invitation; typically clears in 1–3 business days). Required for entry to occupied homes.
5. **Payout setup:** direct-deposit onboarding (via Stripe) — takes ~3 minutes.

**How does priority work?**
Invited vendors of [OPERATOR_NAME] sit first in the dispatch queue for that operator's properties, in their trades and coverage area. A new work order is offered to you for [PRIORITY_WINDOW] before widening to the next vendor. Priority is kept by performance: acceptance rate, show rate, first-time-fix, and resident ratings feed your queue position.

**When do I get paid?**
You mark the job complete with photos → completion is confirmed → payout releases and lands via direct deposit in **[PAYOUT_TIMING, e.g. 2–3 business days]**. No invoicing the operator, no net-30, no chasing checks.

**Do I have to take every job?**
No. Declining a job just passes it down the queue. Chronic non-response lowers your priority position; declining honestly does not carry penalties the way no-showing does.

**Can I get work beyond [OPERATOR_NAME]?**
Yes — once verified you can opt into homeowner jobs and other demand in your service area on the same app and payout rails.

**Who do I contact?**
In-app support, or support@prolnk.xyz — first response within one business day ([SUPPORT_PHONE] for urgent, active-job issues).
