## 2. Condition Documentation (Move-In Shield)

2.1 A photographic condition record of the Premises ("**Move-In Shield Documentation**") will be created at or near move-in, and may be created at move-out, by Resident, Owner/Operator staff, or an authorized service professional using the Platform. The record documents the condition of rooms, fixtures, and systems.

2.2 **Purpose and Resident benefit.** The record establishes pre-existing conditions and is available to Resident in the app, including for use in security-deposit and damage discussions. It serves as [a supplement to / the] inventory and condition form under the Lease. [COUNSEL: in Texas, align with Tex. Prop. Code §92.103–.109 inventory practice.]

2.3 **Consent.** Resident consents to the capture and processing of interior photographs of the Premises for these purposes, and acknowledges that images are processed by the Platform (including AI-assisted analysis) under the Platform's Resident Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Photography is of the Premises and its fixtures — not of persons; identifying content appearing in images (faces, documents, mail, screens) is masked before any use outside the management of Resident's tenancy.

2.4 **Data use.** Resident acknowledges that condition and maintenance data about the Premises, **with Resident's personal information removed and images masked**, may be used and licensed by the Platform in anonymized and aggregated form as described in the Platform Privacy Policy. Resident's identifiable information is not sold.

2.5 Entry for documentation or repairs remains subject to the Lease's entry-and-notice provisions and applicable law.

## 3. Resident App Account

3.1 Resident agrees to register for a Platform account within [DAYS] days of move-in and accept the Platform's Resident Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, which govern Resident's relationship with the Platform. The Platform is an independent contractor of Owner/Operator and is not a party to the Lease.

3.2 Optional services offered in the app that Resident elects to purchase are contracts between Resident and the Platform/service provider, at Resident's own cost. **Resident is never charged for repairs that are Owner/Operator's responsibility** under the Lease or applicable law, and declining optional services does not affect Resident's Lease rights.

3.3 By providing a mobile number, Resident consents to service-related text messages regarding maintenance and scheduling (reply STOP to opt out; opting out of texts does not affect maintenance service — alternate contact will be used).

## KEY DECISIONS FOR COUNSEL

1. **Statutory notice compatibility (§1.3)** — verify per state (Texas first) that app submission counts as legally effective repair notice, and whether the addendum must preserve or recite the statutory written-notice path to protect both parties.
2. **All-adult-occupant signature requirement (§2.3, signature block)** — decide whether photo/data consent must be obtained from every adult occupant (not just leaseholders) for the consent chain supporting the perpetual data license to hold.
3. **Mandatory vs. encouraged channel (§1.1)** — whether requiring the app as the routine channel is enforceable/advisable for residents without smartphones (fair-housing and reasonable-accommodation angle); the [ALTERNATE_CHANNEL] fallback needs to be real.
4. **Move-In Shield as the official inventory form (§2.2)** — electing it as "the" statutory inventory/condition form strengthens the data asset but imports deposit-statute formalities; confirm it satisfies them or keep it as a supplement.
5. **One-page constraint vs. disclosure sufficiency** — this draft compresses data-use disclosure to two sentences (§2.4) and leans on the incorporated Privacy Policy; confirm that incorporation by reference is sufficient consent support in the target states or whether a fuller disclosure block is required.
