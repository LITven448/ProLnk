## KEY DECISIONS FOR COUNSEL

1. **Adoption mechanics for existing pros** — Amendments 4 (2× clawback), 5 (audit), and 6 (perpetual data license) materially increase pro obligations; determine whether in-app continued-use acceptance is enforceable for these or whether affirmative consideration (e.g., access to the new earnings stream in Amendment 1) plus click-through is required.
2. **2× clawback enforceability (Amendment 4)** — validate as liquidated damages (not penalty) under Texas law; assess interaction with restrictive-covenant law for the 12-month tail as applied to independent contractors, and whether the multiplier should scale (e.g., 1× first offense).
3. **FSM data scope (Amendment 3)** — the circumvention-detection purpose (b) implies visibility beyond Platform-originated work orders (to see the off-platform job at all); resolve the tension between "limited to Platform-originated work orders" and effective diversion detection — likely needs a carefully-scoped "Platform-introduced Client match" query right.
4. **Origination-earnings duration (Amendment 1)** — perpetual vs. time-boxed attribution must align with the live commission engine's perpetual 5% home-origination stream and the ≥20% platform-fee floor; perpetual per-record obligations affect unit economics and M&A diligence.
5. **Pro data-license compensation (Amendment 6)** — decide whether pros share in data-licensing revenue (parallel to the operator MSA's tiered share) or origination earnings are sole consideration; inconsistent treatment across the ecosystem invites renegotiation pressure from the founding network.
