# A Dispatch and Vendor Platform Built for CoolSys
### Proposal for a paid design partnership

---

## The gap we think exists

CoolSys runs one of the largest self-performing technician networks in the country — that's the brand, and it's earned. But 110+ branches can't physically cover 45,000 locations across 50 states, so subcontractors fill the gaps. That layer is where the software usually stops.

Work orders arrive from your clients' systems — ServiceChannel, Corrigo, direct. They flow cleanly until the moment a job has to go to an outside vendor. Then it becomes a phone call, an email chain, and a spreadsheet on somebody's branch laptop.

Your Preferred Vendor page already promises those subcontractors a digital platform: electronic work orders, photo documentation, digital completion, expedited payment. **We'd like to help you actually deliver it.**

---

## What we'd build

A white-labeled dispatch and vendor management platform that sits *underneath* your clients' FM systems — not competing with them, completing the last mile.

**Dispatch board.** Every work order in one queue with a live SLA clock. P1 emergencies surface immediately. Assignment in one click to a self-perform tech or a subcontractor, ranked by distance, ETA, trade match, current load, and first-time-fix history.

**Compliance that can't be bypassed.** Every vendor's license, insurance certificate, and EPA 608 certification tracked with expiry dates. A vendor whose COI lapsed **cannot be dispatched** — the system blocks it and tells the dispatcher why. Automated renewal chasing before anything expires.

**Escalation that runs itself.** No acceptance in 15 minutes, the offer widens. 30 minutes, the branch manager is alerted. 60 minutes, the regional director. Every step logged with who and when.

**Vendor registry.** One consolidated list across all 110 branches and every acquired company — trades, coverage, compliance status, performance history. Including a coverage map showing exactly where you have gaps.

**Subcontractor mobile app.** Accept the job, GPS-stamped arrival, guided photo capture, parts against NTE, customer signature, submit. Complete documentation on every job without chasing anyone for paperwork.

**Refrigerant compliance.** EPA Section 608 leak-rate tracking per system, calculated automatically from service records, with repair deadlines flagged before they're missed.

**Executive view.** SLA compliance, cost per work order, days to invoice, and uninsured-dispatch exposure — by branch, by region, by acquisition.

---

## What it's worth to CoolSys

**Getting paid faster.** Complete documentation at job completion means invoices go out days sooner. Every day cut off your collection cycle at your revenue scale frees meaningful working capital — and it compounds across 45,000 locations.

**Lower cost per work order.** Faster dispatch, better vendor matching, and higher first-time-fix rates mean fewer repeat truck rolls. At your volume, small per-order improvements are large annual numbers.

**Contracts protected.** SLA breaches on mission-critical refrigeration are how national accounts get lost. Automated escalation means a job never sits unassigned because someone was on another call.

**Liability closed.** Zero dispatches to uninsured or unlicensed vendors — enforced by the system, not by someone remembering to check a folder.

**Acquisitions integrated in days.** Every company you buy brings a vendor list. Onboarding it becomes a data import instead of a quarter-long project.

**Vendors who stay.** Subcontractors work hardest for whoever pays them fastest and gives them the least paperwork. That's a retention advantage in a tight labor market.

We'd rather prove this on real work than demo it.

**Scope:** one region or one acquired subsidiary · 20–50 active subcontractors · your live work orders
**Duration:** 90 days
**Investment:** $150,000, fixed
**Includes:** platform configuration in CoolSys branding, vendor onboarding, integration with your existing work-order intake, training, and a dedicated team

**What we measure together** — you set the baselines, we agree on them before we start:
- Time from work order received to vendor accepted
- SLA compliance rate
- Days from job completion to invoice submitted
- Percentage of vendors with current compliance documents
- First-time fix rate

**If the numbers don't move, the pilot ends there and you keep the vendor registry we built.** If they do, we discuss a rollout with pilot pricing credited toward it.
