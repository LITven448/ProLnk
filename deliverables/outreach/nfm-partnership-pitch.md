## 2. One-Page Brief

### ProLnk x NFM: Tap-to-Buy Inside AI Room Renderings + Move-In Commerce

**Who we are.** ProLnk (consumer brand: TrustyPro) is an AI-powered home services platform: homeowners and renters photograph their home, our AI understands the space, and we handle everything from repair dispatch to room design. Launching now with a top-5 institutional SFR operator (~60,000 homes) and a DFW consumer rollout.

**The rendering concept — how it works.**
1. A customer photographs their actual living room, bedroom, or dining room in-app.
2. Our AI reconstructs the space — dimensions, lighting, layout, style cues.
3. The AI re-renders the same room, photorealistically furnished — with **NFM products**: real SKUs, correctly scaled using your dimension data, in the customer's real space.
4. Every rendered item is tappable: product name, price, and a buy link straight into NFM checkout.

It is not a generic mood board. It is *their* room, furnished with *your* inventory, one tap from purchase.

**The move-in channel.** Through our SFR operator program, we're present at lease signing and move-in across a large Texas rental portfolio. Move-in is the highest-intent furniture moment in retail; today NFM competes for it with billboards and search ads. We put NFM in the move-in workflow itself — "here's your new home, furnished."

**Why NFM specifically.** Your Texas store is the highest-volume home-furnishings location in the country and our launch market is Dallas–Fort Worth. Geography, delivery logistics, and brand strength all line up. We want one anchor furniture partner in DFW — we're offering NFM first position.

**The ask.**
- Product catalog feed: images (high-res, ideally with transparent/3D assets where available), dimensions, SKUs, live pricing and availability.
- Affiliate commission on attributed sales.

**The offer.**
- Default placement of NFM products in AI room renderings for DFW-market users.
- Move-in commerce placement across the Texas rental portfolio program.
- Pay-for-performance only: no placement fees, no media commitment.

### Anticipated Objections — and Answers

**"We can't control how our brand and products appear in AI renders."**
Agreed — so you get control. Proposal: (a) an approval workflow where NFM signs off on rendering style templates and product-presentation rules before launch; (b) renders are watermarked and clearly labeled as AI visualizations, never presented as product photography; (c) products render only with accurate SKU imagery, dimensions, and current pricing from your own feed; (d) kill-switch: NFM can pull any SKU or category from renderings at any time via the feed.

**"Returns will be a mess — a render isn't the real product."**
Every render carries an explicit "AI visualization" label, and the tap-through lands on your real product page — the purchase decision happens on NFM.com with your photography, specs, and policies. Commission is on net sales (returns clawed back in monthly reconciliation), inside a last-touch [30]-day attribution window, so NFM never pays commission on returned goods.

**"We don't do affiliate programs."**
Exactly why this is a direct, bespoke agreement — one partner, one market, performance-only economics, full brand control, 12-month pilot with clean exit. It's closer to a shoppable-media pilot than an affiliate network listing.

**"Why not build this ourselves?"**
The rendering tech isn't the moat — the distribution is: the SFR move-in channel and the home-services user base photographing their rooms for other reasons. We already have the room photos and the moments; NFM has the inventory. Neither works alone.
