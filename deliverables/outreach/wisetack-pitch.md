## 2. One-Page Partnership Brief

### ProLnk x Wisetack: Embedded Financing on an AI-Verified Job Platform

**What ProLnk is.** An AI-powered home services marketplace. Consumer flow: photo of the problem → AI diagnosis and scope → dispatch of a verified professional → escrowed payment released on verified completion. Consumer brand: TrustyPro.

**Where we are.**
- Rental-operator program launching with a top-5 institutional SFR operator (~60,000 single-family homes) — recurring maintenance, turns, and capex-adjacent repair volume.
- Consumer homeowner launch in DFW immediately following, expanding from the same contractor supply base.

**Why ProLnk is a better book than a typical FSM integration.**

| Typical FSM merchant | ProLnk job |
|---|---|
| Free-text estimate, scope quality varies by contractor | Every job AI-scoped from photos before dispatch — structured scope, category, severity, and price band attached to the financing application context |
| Contractor invoices; platform has limited visibility into completion | Payment escrowed; release gated on completion verification (photos, sign-off) — materially lower fraud and non-completion risk on funded jobs |
| Open merchant onboarding | Verified contractors only — licensing/insurance/background screening before first dispatch |
| Mixed ticket sizes, heavy small-job volume | Big-ticket categories are core: roofing, HVAC, foundation — the segment the LendingClub $65K expansion targets |

**Integration ask.** Standard Wisetack embedded API inside the ProLnk quote/checkout flow: prequalification at the point the AI-generated scope and price band is presented (before the homeowner commits), financing offer alongside the escrow payment option.

**What we bring.** A concentrated DFW launch market, a two-sided funnel (operator-driven resident jobs + consumer homeowner jobs), and job-level data quality (photos + structured AI scope + verified completion) that should improve approval quality and reduce losses on funded volume.

**Proposed next step.** Technical scoping call + commercial term sheet targeting integration live for the DFW consumer launch.

## 3. Eight Questions to Ask Wisetack

1. **Revenue share:** What rev-share or per-funded-loan economics do software platform partners receive on funded volume, and how does it scale with volume tiers?
2. **Approval-rate SLAs:** What approval rates do comparable home-services platforms see, and will you commit to approval-rate or offer-rate benchmarks/SLAs given our richer job data (photos + AI scope)?
3. **Underwriting data:** Can our structured job data (AI scope, category, price band, completion verification) feed underwriting or pricing — and does better data earn us better terms or higher approvals?
4. **Second-look lenders:** Beyond LendingClub, what waterfall / second-look lender options exist for declined applicants, and can we add one?
5. **Integration timeline:** Typical API integration timeline and engineering lift for a platform partner, and what's needed to be live for a DFW launch date of [DATE]?
6. **Loan range and categories:** Confirm the $65K ceiling applies to our categories (roofing, HVAC, foundation) and any category-level restrictions, term lengths, and promo (0% / same-as-cash) options.
7. **Exclusivity of placement:** What placement terms do you require — is Wisetack asking for exclusive financing placement in our checkout, and what do we get in exchange (better economics, marketing support, co-launch)?
8. **Merchant onboarding at scale:** Since financing is offered merchant-side, how do you onboard our verified contractor base in bulk (KYB, merchant agreements), and can ProLnk act as the master merchant/platform of record?
